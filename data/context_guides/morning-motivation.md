# Morning Motivation

To start the morning with motivation, reach for bright, uplifting tracks with a
positive lift and just enough energy to get you going. Sunny pop, jangly indie
pop, and warm funk all set an optimistic tone, with cheerful hooks, crisp drums,
and a spring in the tempo. Think sunrise rather than midnight: warm, hopeful, and
ready to move, but not so intense that it overwhelms a slow start. A good morning
track wakes you up gently and then nudges the day forward, building momentum as
the coffee kicks in.
