# Romantic and Intimate

For a romantic, intimate evening, a slow dinner or a quiet night in, choose warm,
tender, soulful music. Smooth r&b, classic soul, and late-night jazz all lean
into closeness, with expressive vocals, brushed drums, warm bass, and unhurried
tempos. Let the arrangements stay close and unhurried, the kind of sound that
fills a small room without crowding a conversation. Keep the mood gentle and the
volume low. The music should feel like candlelight: present, tender, and never
demanding the spotlight.
