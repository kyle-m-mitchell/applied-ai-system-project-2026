# Workout and Energy

For a workout, pick high-energy, fast, intense tracks that keep you moving.
Cardio sessions, gym sets, and a hard run thrive on driving momentum and a
powerful, propulsive beat, so lean toward genres built for force: metal, punk,
edm, and hard-charging rock all deliver relentless tempo and loud, aggressive
energy. Look for pounding drums, distorted guitars, and a pulse fast enough to
set your pace. When your legs want to quit, loud and relentless is exactly the
point. Save the calm, quiet material for the cool-down stretch afterward; here
the job of the music is to push your exercise a little harder than you would on
your own.
