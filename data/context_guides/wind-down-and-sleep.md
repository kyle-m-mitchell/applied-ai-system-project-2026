# Wind Down and Sleep

To relax and unwind at the end of the day, choose soft, quiet, slow music that
lowers the tempo of the room. Warm ambient textures, gentle classical, and the
calmest lofi drift toward sleep without ever jolting you back awake. Favor long,
spacious pads, soft piano, and low volume over anything busy or bright; sparse is
better than full, and slow is better than steady. Keep it calm and unhurried,
with nothing that invites you to get back up. Think of it as dimming the lights in
sound, easing you gently toward bedtime.
