# Party and Dance Floor

For a party or a packed dance floor, choose upbeat, danceable tracks with an
irresistible groove. Club, hip-hop, pop, house, and funk grooves, bright hooks,
and a steady four-on-the-floor pulse keep a celebration moving and a room on its
feet. Reach for punchy drums, deep bass, and big, singable choruses that a crowd
can shout back. Lean into momentum: each track should hand energy to the next
rather than letting the dancing cool. Tempo and confidence matter more than
subtlety here. Look for tracks that capture the life of the party and keep the
floor full.
