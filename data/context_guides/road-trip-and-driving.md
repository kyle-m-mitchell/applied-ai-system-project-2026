# Road Trip and Driving

A road trip wants momentum. For long driving stretches on the highway, and for
the daily commute alike, pick tracks with forward motion and a bright, sing-along
spirit. Anthemic rock, indie pop, open-road country, and pulsing synthwave all
carry that sense of movement, with steady drums and hooks made for the windows
down. Aim for enough energy and motivation to eat the miles without wearing you
out, and choruses big enough to sing over the engine. The best driving music
feels like the scenery moving past the window, matching the rhythm of the road.
