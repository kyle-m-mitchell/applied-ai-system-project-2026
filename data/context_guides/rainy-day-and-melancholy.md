# Rainy Day and Melancholy

Some days call for reflective, melancholy music rather than a mood-lift. On a
rainy afternoon, a somber piano, an aching blues guitar, or a slow, introspective
ballad can feel honest and comforting. Genres that sit with a feeling do this
best: blues, spare classical, and slow, tender soul carry weight without rushing
you out of it. Listen for unhurried tempos, minor keys, warm strings, and vocals
that sound close and worn-in. This is music to sit with a feeling, not to erase
it. If a low or sad mood lingers well beyond the weather, a playlist is no
substitute for talking to someone you trust.
